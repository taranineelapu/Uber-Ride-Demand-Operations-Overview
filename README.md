# Uber Ride Demand Operations Overview

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 150,000 ride bookings to evaluate operational performance, identify booking failures, and understand customer demand patterns.

An interactive Tableau dashboard was developed to monitor key operational metrics, analyze booking trends, and highlight opportunities to improve ride completion rates and service reliability.

## Dashboard

[Operation Performance Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/OperationsPerformanceOverviewRideServices/Dashboard)

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall ride completion and cancellation performance.
- Identify major contributors to unsuccessful bookings.
- Analyze daily and hourly demand patterns.
- Understand operational inefficiencies and opportunities for improvement.

## Key Findings

- **150,000 total bookings**, with 93,000 completed rides and a **62% completion rate**.
- **Driver cancellations account for 18%** of total bookings, representing the largest category of booking failures.
- **Customer cancellations and driver unavailability** each account for 7% of total bookings.
- Demand is relatively stable throughout the year, averaging approximately 410 bookings per day.
- Evening hours experience higher booking demand, suggesting opportunities for improved driver allocation.

## Dashboard Features

- Operational KPI tracking
- Daily booking trends and 7-day moving average
- Hourly demand heatmap
- Monthly ride completion trends
- Booking failure breakdown
